# Approval policy

## Green — execute without a separate confirmation

Read/search operations, internal tasks, internal notes, and drafts that are not sent externally. Summarize what changed afterward.

## Yellow — require Dominic's explicit confirmation immediately before execution

Creating a Jobber request, sending a customer email or text, rescheduling a visit, or changing a customer record. Show the target, exact proposed change/message, and likely impact. Approval is one-time and applies only to the displayed action.

## Red — never execute through the normal flow

Sending an invoice, issuing a refund, changing pricing, canceling a job, deleting a record, or bulk messaging. Prepare a review packet and require both an explicitly enabled red-tool setting and a fresh confirmation. Never broaden an approval from one record to a batch.

## Verification

An accepted Zapier request means queued, not completed. Report success only after a downstream result or source-of-truth record confirms it. If execution status is unknown, say so and leave the work item open.

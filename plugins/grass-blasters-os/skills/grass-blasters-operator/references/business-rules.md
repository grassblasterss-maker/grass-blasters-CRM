# Grass Blasters business rules

- Company: Grass Blasters LLC.
- Operating area: Akron, Canton, North Canton, and surrounding service areas. Verify the exact ZIP/service boundary from Settings before rejecting a lead.
- Lead response target: 15 minutes during business hours.
- Quote follow-up target: 3 days after sent, unless the customer replied or asked for a later date.
- High-value opportunity: above $5,000.
- Large opportunity: above $10,000.
- Invoice escalation warning: 30 or more days overdue.
- Customer communication style: professional, concise, friendly, and specific about the next step.
- Normal Jobber lifecycle: client → request → quote → job. Do not skip directly to job for an unqualified lead.
- Never create a duplicate Jobber client. Search phone, then email, then name/company.
- Preserve Facebook lead ID, campaign, ad set, ad, form, service, ZIP, and resulting Jobber IDs.
- Keep customer/job facts in Jobber. Keep only operating state and cross-system metadata in Zapier Tables.
